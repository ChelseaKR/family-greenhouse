## Notes

1. **This report describes the product after the fixes on branch
   `a11y/acr-2026-11`.** That branch is not released yet. The statements below
   are true of the live product only once that branch is merged and deployed
   in a release after 0.53.2. Until then, the rows that name a fix in their
   remarks describe the code on the branch, not the live site.
2. **Scope.** The conformance tables cover:
   - the public web site: home, pricing, care guides and pet-safe pages, blog,
     help, legal, support, status, changelog and gift pages;
   - the signed-in web app: dashboard, plants, plant detail and passport, add
     and import plants, tasks, Today, household, settings (all sections),
     analytics, chat, help, onboarding, away recap and the caretaker report;
   - the account-free token pages: plant-sitter page and brief, shared cutting
     card, plant tag scan page, kiosk page, household invite;
   - the HTML emails built by `backend/src/services/email/template.ts`. The
     daily reminder, weekly digest, year recap and welcome email were rendered
     in both languages and scanned; the trial lifecycle emails and the email
     outage notice use the same template and blocks and were checked by
     reading their composition code, not rendered. The unsubscribe pages
     (plain HTML with a heading, a paragraph and a real button) were checked
     by code reading.
     Billing, household, invite and checkout-recovery emails are plain text
     only, which has no visual presentation to fail, and are not listed per
     criterion.

   The iOS app shows the same web app inside a native frame (tab bar,
   navigation bar, intro screens, form sheets). The web content is covered by
   the tables. **The native frame was not run on a device or simulator for
   this report.** Its status is in
   [Appendix C](#appendix-c-ios-app-native-frame), outside the conformance
   tables, so that nothing in the tables rests on evidence that was not
   gathered. The Android app is not in scope.

3. **The web app has one colour theme.** It does not follow the system dark
   mode, and the iOS app sets `UIUserInterfaceStyle` to Light. The 22 screens of
   this report's extra sweep were also scanned with
   `prefers-color-scheme: dark` emulated; they render the same, with the same
   results. The HTML emails do have a dark
   variant, and both variants were checked.
4. **All evidence is automated or synthetic.** No screen reader, voice
   control or switch was used, and no person walked the product without a
   pointer or at real browser zoom; the keyboard and zoom results come from
   scripts. Rows whose result depends on what assistive technology announces
   open their remarks with **"Qualified: synthetic evidence; human AT review
   pending"**, say what was verified (the accessibility tree, live regions,
   ARIA state), and name the step in
   [Appendix B](#appendix-b-human-walkthrough) that still needs a person with
   VoiceOver or NVDA. Those rows are not unqualified `Supports` (standard §2.0),
   and any failure the walkthrough finds will change their level.
5. **Spanish.** The product is offered in English and Spanish. Spanish screens
   were covered by the repo's existing unit tests and the November 2026
   accessibility sweep, not re-scanned page by page for this report.

## Evaluation methods used

- **axe-core 4 through Playwright** (`@axe-core/playwright`), tags `wcag2a`,
  `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa`, `wcag22aa`, in Chromium:
  - the repo's own suites `frontend/tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts` and
    `a11y-authenticated.spec.ts` (public, token and signed-in routes, settings
    sections, the command palette open, the plant passport on screen and in
    print);
  - an additional sweep for this report of 24 screens not in those suites or
    worth a second look (a blog post, a help topic, gift, confirm email, reset
    password, not found, away recap, caretaker report, onboarding, the mobile
    navigation drawer open, and others), 22 of them in both light
    and emulated dark colour scheme: 46 scans, **0 WCAG A/AA violations**;
  - all eight rendered HTML emails (four kinds, English and Spanish) in light
    and dark: 0 violations.
- **Two new committed axe scans** for the populated plant tag and kiosk pages, which were previously scanned only in their invalid-link state. The kiosk scan found one contrast failure, fixed on the branch.
- **Keyboard walk.** For 22 screens at 1280 px and 390 px wide, and the plant page at 1280 px, Tab was
  pressed through up to 50 stops while recording, for each focused element,
  whether it had a visible indicator (outline or ring) and whether anything
  else was painted over its centre: 1,491 stops, none without an indicator,
  none covered. The repo's `keyboard-path.spec.ts` (sign-in, skip link,
  completing a task by keyboard) passed.
- **Reflow.** The repo's `reflow.spec.ts` (320 × 256 CSS px) passed; the HTML
  emails were also measured at 320 px: no horizontal scrolling.
- **Text spacing.** The WCAG 1.4.12 overrides (line height 1.5, letter
  spacing 0.12 em, word spacing 0.16 em, paragraph spacing 2 em) were injected
  on 23 screens and the 8 emails, and every element that clipped its own text was listed.
- **Target size.** Every control's box was measured on the same screens;
  undersized ones were checked against the 2.5.8 spacing and inline
  exceptions, which axe's `target-size` rule also evaluates.
- **Contrast.** axe's `color-contrast` rule on every scan, plus the token
  pairs computed by hand (below) for the cases axe marks "needs review" and for
  non-text contrast, which axe does not measure.
- **Unit tests.** `jest-axe` on shared components, the nested-live-region and
  English-only-page checks, and new tests for each fix on the branch (each
  fails on the code before the fix).
- **Lint.** `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y` at `strict`.
- **Code reading** for what automation cannot see: focus management, time
  limits, gestures, authentication, error handling, consistent help, and the
  iOS native frame's SwiftUI and UIKit code.
- **The November 2026 sweep** (`fix/a11y-sweep-2026-11`, merged as #1165 and
  #1166): keyboard and accessibility-tree checks in Chromium and WebKit,
  forced colours, and Spanish. Its open findings are carried into the remarks
  below.

Not used: screen readers, voice control, switch access, Windows High Contrast
on real hardware, Firefox, a physical iPhone or the iOS simulator, the
Android app.

## Terms

- **Supports**: the functionality of the product has at least one method that
  meets the criterion without known defects or meets with equivalent
  facilitation.
- **Partially Supports**: some functionality of the product does not meet the
  criterion.
- **Does Not Support**: the majority of product functionality does not meet
  the criterion.
- **Not Applicable**: the criterion is not relevant to the product.
- **Not Evaluated**: the product has not been evaluated against the
  criterion. ITI allows this term for Level AAA only, so it is not used in the
  tables below; it is used in Appendix C, which is outside them.

## Appendix B: Human walkthrough

To be done by a person, with the release that carries this branch, on
production or staging. About 3.5 hours in all.

1. **Set up (15 min).** macOS Safari with VoiceOver (Cmd+F5), and Windows
   Firefox or Chrome with NVDA. A test household with two members, a few
   plants with photos, overdue and upcoming tasks.
2. **Sign up and first plant (30 min, both screen readers).** Register,
   confirm the email code (paste it), add the first plant with a photo.
   Listen for: each field's label and error; the photo upload saying it
   started and finished; the page title read after each step; focus never
   jumping to the top of the page.
3. **Daily use (30 min, VoiceOver).** Dashboard, Tasks, complete a task by
   keyboard, snooze one, open the command palette (Cmd+K) and search. Listen
   for: each toast read once; "Plants" and "Tasks" read as groups in the
   palette results; the selected filter chip read as pressed; nothing read
   twice.
4. **Analytics and plant page (15 min, NVDA).** Listen for the 30-day chart's
   total and then each day's count; photo names; the photo timeline reachable
   and scrollable by arrow keys.
5. **Settings, Notifications (15 min, both).** Turn email off. Listen for:
   "Weekly plant digest, switch, off, dimmed" followed by the reason; the
   household-email switches announced as a group named "Household emails".
6. **Chat and household (20 min, VoiceOver).** Ask a question; the reply should
   be read once, when complete. Make a member admin, generate an invite link,
   set a vacation: focus should land on what each control turned into.
7. **Token pages (15 min, VoiceOver on an iPhone).** Scan a plant tag QR code,
   mark the task done; open a sitter link and a kiosk link. Listen for headings
   and the completion being announced.
8. **Emails (15 min).** Open a reminder and a digest in Apple Mail with
   VoiceOver and in Outlook on the web with NVDA. Listen for: the title read as
   a heading, section labels as headings (VO+Cmd+H to move between them), the
   logo read as "Family Greenhouse", each plant link's name.
9. **iOS app native frame (30 min, VoiceOver on an iPhone).** See Appendix C
   for what to check.
10. **Keyboard only and real zoom (30 min).** Put the mouse away and do step 2
    and step 3 again with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, Escape and the arrow
    keys only, then in Chrome at 200% and 400% browser zoom. Look for: a
    visible focus ring on every stop, nothing focused hidden under the header
    or a toast, no trap, no horizontal scrolling, nothing cut off.
11. **Forced colours (10 min).** Windows High Contrast: every control keeps an
    edge, the selected chip and toggle are visible, focus is visible.
12. **Record** the results in a dated
    `docs/a11y/screen-reader-walkthrough-YYYY-MM-DD.md` (standard §2), mark
    each failure against its criterion here, remove the "Qualified" prefix
    from rows the walkthrough confirms, and change "Status of this report" to
    issued with that date.
